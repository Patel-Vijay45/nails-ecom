# 0. Market Research: Business Findings

*Researched November 2026. Market-report numbers differ a lot between publishers because each defines the market differently, so treat every figure as a range, not an exact number. Sources are listed at the end.*

## Summary

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Is the market big enough?** | Yes. India's nail care market is about **USD 0.7–1.2 billion (₹6,000–10,000 crore)** a year and is growing **~4–10% a year**. |
| **Is press-on nails a real category?** | Yes. Worldwide it is roughly **USD 0.8–1.8 billion**, growing **~6.5–7.7% a year**, and **Asia Pacific is the fastest-growing region**. Most sales happen **online**. |
| **Is India buying online?** | Yes. India's beauty & personal care market is about **USD 28–31 billion**, and **10–30% of it is sold online**. Online and quick-commerce beauty sales grew **39%** in one year (2024). |
| **Where are the customers?** | **Instagram.** India has the most Instagram users in the world (**~390–480 million**), and the largest age group is **18–24**. |
| **Is there competition?** | Yes, but it is **mass-produced**: ₹169–₹1,499 sets on Nykaa and Amazon. A funded D2C press-on brand (Nailinit) launched in 2024, which shows investors believe in the category. **Very few sellers offer artist-made, made-to-measure, or bridal custom sets.** |
| **Our opportunity** | Be the **"real nail artist" brand**: handmade designs, exact-size sets, and bridal/festive custom work. Mass brands can't copy these. |

### India beauty & personal care (the wider pool)

- **USD 28–31 billion in 2025**, expected to reach **~USD 49 billion by 2034** (IMARC, Ken Research).
- **Online share: 10–30%**, depending on the source and definition.
- **E-commerce + quick-commerce beauty sales grew 39%** in value (June–November 2024 vs the year before).
- India's D2C (direct-to-consumer brand) market is about **USD 12 billion**, and several D2C brands crossed ₹100 crore revenue within 3–5 years.

## 2. Demand drivers in India

1. **Weddings.** India holds about **46 lakh weddings a year** (CAIT, 2025), worth about **₹6.5 lakh crore** in spending. Every wedding has a bride plus sisters, cousins and friends at mehendi, sangeet and reception. Even **0.01% of weddings = ~460 bridal orders a year.**
2. **Festivals.** Diwali, Karwa Chauth, Teej, Eid, Navratri, Raksha Bandhan, Christmas and New Year all create recurring "new look" moments.
3. **Instagram culture.** India has the largest Instagram audience in the world, and it skews young (18–24 is the biggest group). Nail art is a very visual product that does well in Reels.
4. **Convenience and cost.** A salon gel or extension visit costs ₹1,000–3,000 and takes 1–2 hours. Press-ons take 10 minutes at home, are reusable, and are easy to remove, which suits working women and students.
5. **Workplace rules.** Many offices, schools and hospitals don't allow long nails. Press-ons can go on for a weekend and come off on Monday.

| Level | Meaning | Estimate |

| **TAM**: total market | All nail care spending in India | **~₹6,000–10,000 crore/year** |
| **SAM**: market we can serve | Artificial/press-on nails bought online in India, plus bridal/festive nail looks. No public figure exists. **Our estimate is low hundreds of crores per year** | ~₹100–500 crore/year *(our estimate, not from a report)* |
| **SOM**: what we can realistically win | One artist plus one helper, selling online | See below |

### Realistic revenue for one artist (our planning numbers)

| Stage | Orders / month | Avg order value | Revenue / month | Revenue / year |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Start (months 1–3) | 20–50 | ₹900 | ₹18k–45k | — |
| Growing (months 4–12) | 100–200 | ₹1,000 | ₹1–2 lakh | ₹12–24 lakh |
| With a helper and bridal season | 300+ | ₹1,100 | ₹3.3 lakh+ | ₹40 lakh+ |

At ~65–75% gross margin (see the [business plan](01-business-plan.md)), the "growing" stage is a solid side business, and the "helper" stage is a full-time business.

**Limit to plan for:** one artist can only make so many sets. To grow past ~300 orders a month you will need to (a) batch-produce best-sellers, (b) train a helper for simple designs, and (c) keep the artist's time for premium custom and bridal work.

## 5. Opportunities and risks

### Opportunities
- **Bridal and festive custom sets.** High price, low competition, and brides spend freely.
- **Made-to-measure.** Solves the #1 press-on problem (bad fit) that mass brands ignore.
- **Personal brand.** Customers buy from a person, and "Made by [artist name]" can't be copied.
- **Repeat buying.** Press-ons are reusable but people want new designs for every occasion. Monthly design drops keep customers coming back.
- **Later:** export to the Indian diaspora (UK, US, UAE, Canada) for weddings, and wholesale to boutiques and salons.

### Risks
| Risk | How we handle it |
|------|------------------|
| Cheap ₹199–299 sets on Amazon | Don't compete on price. Compete on design, fit, and story. |
| Fit problems → returns | Sizing kit, saved nail sizes on the website, extra sizes in every box |
| Cash-on-delivery returns | Prepaid discount, COD confirmation on WhatsApp |
| Production limit (one artist) | Batch best-sellers, hire a helper, limit custom slots |
| Funded competitors (e.g. Nailinit) | Own the handmade, custom, and bridal space they don't focus on |

## 6. What this means for the website

These findings shaped the store we're building:
- **Mobile-first, Instagram-style design**, because customers arrive from Reels on their phones.
- **Shape / length / size picker + saved nail sizes**, to fix the fit problem.
- **Sizing kit as a product**, to make the first purchase low-risk.
- **Custom / bridal order form** with event date and reference photos.
- **UPI + COD at checkout**, plus WhatsApp order updates, because that is how India pays and talks.
- **Admin panel**, so new designs, prices and drops can go live in minutes without code.
