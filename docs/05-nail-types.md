# 5. Nail Types: Research and What the Website Supports

*Researched November 2026 from beauty and retailer guides (sources at the end). Names vary a little between brands.*

## 5.1 Nail shapes (11)

| Shape | What it looks like | Who it suits | On the website |
|-------|--------------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Square** | Straight sides, flat tip, sharp corners | Short nails, wide nail beds, French tips | ✅ |
| **Squoval** | Square with softened, rounded corners | Everyone; strong and low-maintenance | ✅ |
| **Round** | Follows the fingertip curve, short | Short nails, office, very practical | ✅ |
| **Oval** | Longer rounded tip, egg shape | Makes fingers look longer and slimmer | ✅ |
| **Almond** | Slim sides tapering to a soft rounded point | Most-requested shape; elegant, bridal | ✅ |
| **Coffin / Ballerina** | Tapered sides, flat squared tip | Medium to long glam sets | ✅ |
| **Stiletto** | Long, sharp point | Bold, party and editorial looks | ✅ |

## 5.3 Width sizes

Press-on sizes are based on **width**, not length, and **every brand's chart is different**. Some brands label sizes 0–9 or 0–11 (0 is widest), others XS–XL. This is why the website uses the shop's **own** tip widths (Admin → Settings) for photo sizing, plus XS/S/M/L for ready-made sets.

## 5.4 Nail types (systems)

### Products we can sell online

| Type | What it is | Wear time | Reusable | On the website (product "type") |
|------|------------|-----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Soft gel press-on** | Flexible gel tips, painted and cured; most natural look | 1–3 weeks with glue | Yes, 3–5× | ✅ Soft gel |
| **Acrylic press-on** | Harder, very durable tips; good for long or 3D designs | 2–3 weeks with glue | Yes | ✅ Acrylic |
| **ABS press-on** | Budget plastic tips, mass-produced | Days to 1 week | Sometimes | ✅ ABS |
| **Semi-cured gel strips** | Gel "stickers" that harden under a UV/LED lamp | ~2 weeks | No | ✅ Gel strips |
| **Nail stickers / wraps** | Printed polish stickers, no lamp needed | 3–7 days | No | ✅ Nail stickers |
| **Kits & accessories** | Sizing kit, glue, tabs, prep and removal kits, cuticle oil | — | — | ✅ Kit / Accessory |

### Salon techniques (offline services, not sold online)

| Technique | What it is | Notes |
|-----------|------------|-------|
| **Acrylic extensions** | Liquid + powder sculpted on the nail | Strongest for long nails; fills every ~2 weeks |
| **Gel-X / soft gel extensions** | Full-cover soft gel tips glued with gel and cured | Lighter than acrylic |
| **Hard gel** | Sculpted gel, filed to shape | Removed by filing |
| **Polygel (acrygel)** | Gel + acrylic hybrid from a tube | Not for very long extensions |
| **Builder gel / BIAB** | Strengthening overlay on natural nails | Helps weak nails grow |
| **Dip powder** | Nail dipped into coloured powder over a bonding liquid | Hybrid of gel and acrylic |
| **Gel polish** | Colour cured under a lamp | 2–3 weeks |

Improperly applied or removed gel and acrylic can cause allergies, infections or nail damage. That's a key reason customers like press-ons.
